I. The shirt

A shirt of pure cotton.

Two-fold Egyptian poplin, collar cut to the line of your jaw.

II. The waistcoat

Measured, then held close.

Five buttons, a silk back, and a cinch that sits exactly at your natural waist.

III. The jacket

Built on full canvas.

Horsehair, hand-padded lapels, a chest that moulds to you over years of wear.

IV. The cloth

Woven in Yorkshire.

Super 130s worsted, pattern-matched across every seam and pocket flap.

V. The detail

Working cuffs, finished by hand.

Four kissing horn buttons. Cufflinks of gold and onyx.
